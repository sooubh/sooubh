GULLY CRICKET

Privacy Policy — Last updated: April 12, 2025

Effective Date: April 12, 2025

Short version: Gully Cricket is primarily an offline app. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Ads are shown only when you have internet access via Google AdMob. All match data stays on your device.

1. Who We Are

Gully Cricket is developed by Sooubh (Developer), an independent developer based in India. Contact: sourabh3527@gmail.com

Package name: com.sooubh.gullycricket

2. What Data We Collect

We collect as little as possible. Here is a full breakdown:

Data Type Collected? Purpose
Player names you enter ✅ Stored locally Match scoring, saved players list
Match scores & history ✅ Stored locally Match history, statistics
App preferences ✅ Stored locally Theme, rules, settings
Device identifier ⚠️ AdMob only Ad serving (only with internet)
IP address ⚠️ Local WiFi only WiFi hotspot multiplayer (never sent to servers)
Name, email, phone ❌ Never Not collected
Location ❌ Never Not collected
Camera / microphone ❌ Never recorded Camera used only for QR scan (not stored)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • All match data, player names, and statistics are stored only on your device using Hive local database.
  • No data is uploaded to any server.
  • WiFi multiplayer works entirely on your local network — scores are shared device-to-device only, not through the internet.
  • When you delete matches or uninstall the app, all local data is removed.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Gully Cricket shows ads only when your device has an active internet connection. We use Google AdMob to serve ads.

  • When offline: no ads are shown, no ad network is contacted.
  • When online: AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show relevant ads.
  • AdMob's data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Ad types used: Banner ads and Interstitial (full-screen) ads.

To opt out of personalized ads, visit: google.com/settings/ads

5. Permissions We Request

Permission Why We Need It
INTERNET To load AdMob ads when connected to internet
ACCESS_WIFI_STATE To get device WiFi IP for local multiplayer hosting
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE To detect if internet is available (ads on/off)
CAMERA To scan QR codes for joining multiplayer matches (not stored)
VIBRATE Haptic feedback on score buttons
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Live score notifications during active match
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Keep live score notification active during match
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Optional floating score overlay over other apps (user-granted only)

6. Children's Privacy

Gully Cricket does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is a sports scoring tool suitable for all ages. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it immediately.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Google AdMob — for ad serving (only when internet connected).
  • Legal requirements — if required by law.

8. Data Security

All data is stored locally on your device using Hive database. No cloud storage or remote servers are used. The security of your device protects your data.

9. Multiplayer Data Sharing

When using WiFi multiplayer mode:

  • Match score data is shared between devices on the same local WiFi network only.
  • No data leaves your local network.
  • Other devices in the match receive a copy of the match data and store it locally on their device.
  • No player names or match data is sent to our servers (we have none).

10. Your Rights

  • Delete data: Uninstall the app to delete all local data. Within the app, use Settings → Clear All Data.
  • Access data: All your data is viewable within the app (Match History, Player Stats).
  • Opt-out of ads: Turn off internet / use in offline mode to see no ads.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
